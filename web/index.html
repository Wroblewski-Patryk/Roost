Roost

Roost is a self-hosted private operating workspace for tasks, documents, knowledge, decisions and supervised agents.

Connecting Google Drive lets authorized users synchronize selected folders, read document and spreadsheet content, and create, edit, rename, move, trash or restore supported files. Roost stores file metadata and content snapshots to support workspace search and two-way synchronization. Connected ClickUp lists provide task and comment synchronization.

Workspace information is available to authorized members and enabled agents for approved work. See our privacy policy for details about access, use, storage, sharing and removal of connected data.
