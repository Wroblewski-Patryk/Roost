# Company operating workflows

This document translates the accepted product requirements into owner-visible work journeys. It is a design and delivery map, not evidence that a route or database table works and not a second gate queue. `requirements.md` remains the acceptance registry; `../implementation.md` records the current delivery order and proof. The workflows apply to a configured company, not to Roost's own development by its connected Worker.

## Shared operating model

The owner starts from **work requiring action**, not a directory of departments or database entities. Every active item answers: what outcome is sought, why it matters, which customer/product/application/goal it belongs to, who owns the next action, what blocks it, and what evidence will close it. A linked record can be reached from its customer, project, task, or attention item without reconstructing the story from separate tables. Current work, drafts, completed history, and archived records are distinct views.

Use existing canonical records and links before adding storage. The intended relationships are:

`company purpose -> measurable goal -> product or service outcome -> application/engagement/project -> milestone -> task -> result/evidence -> review/acceptance -> measured outcome and cost`.

A procedure is the reusable **method**, not a goal or a completed job. A decision is an unresolved or answered **question requiring an authorized choice**, not a substitute for a task, test, or status entry. One person or agent profile owns a next action; execution identity, model, review and cost are recorded honestly. Provider records remain owned by the declared provider, with Roost showing their links and synchronization state.

## Journeys to make usable

| Journey | Owner entry and required path | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily work | One home shows only actionable questions, blocked or overdue work, results awaiting review, incidents and material opportunities. Each item opens the underlying record and its next action. Routine technical choices stay with an authorized worker. | The owner can identify and finish one real action without searching departments; history and routine events do not crowd the queue. RF-OUT-001/002, RF-UX-001/002. |
| Direction and goals | Purpose is the qualitative direction. A goal has an outcome, baseline or explicit unknown, measure and source, target, accountable person, review cadence, linked projects/tasks, actual progress and explanation of variance. Project milestones remain distinct from company goals. | The owner can explain why a goal exists, how progress is measured, which work contributes, what is off track and who acts next. No bootstrap or site-test goal appears as an active business goal. RF-OUT-007/008, RF-ORG-002. |
| Questions and decisions | A material blocker becomes one plain question with context, affected record, options, recommendation, consequences, deferral and an exact action. Answering updates only the authorized scope. Corrections retain audit history behind the record; they are not new daily questions. | The owner can answer or defer a real question once, see the affected work update, and find its history separately. RF-CTX-016/017/018, RF-OUT-003. |
| Procedures and repeatable work | Per department, an owner can find an applicable versioned procedure with trigger, inputs, roles, ordered steps, outputs, acceptance, exception and improvement path. Launching a procedure against a project/customer/application previews and creates one traceable run and the required linked tasks; a repeated launch cannot silently duplicate them. Each task records the pinned procedure version, responsible human/agent/script, result and evidence. | A real procedure creates the intended tasks, their statuses roll up to the run, and a diagnosed improvement can produce a reviewed new version without rewriting an in-flight run. RF-ORG-002, RF-CTX-012/020/026/029. |
| Existing application takeover | Register one application and canonical repo/deployment. Recover accepted assumptions and current observed state; classify verified capability, defect, unfinished function, missing assumption and stale documentation. Resolve contradictions into a baseline, then plan bounded repairs, evidence and readiness. | The owner sees proven stage, critical blockers, next outcome, accountable actor and linked evidence; a completed repair changes the stage only when acceptance criteria pass. RF-APP-003/010/014. |
| New application or website | Begin with problem, intended user, core value and scope; create accepted requirements and solution design before project tasks. Follow the same lifecycle as takeover from the proven starting stage. Generate work from a reviewed project/procedure template while preserving application-specific choices and change control. | A new application has a traceable path from need to build, verification, handover and later sale/operation without copying another app's assumptions. RF-APP-002/010/012/013. |
| People and agents | One installed Windows companion connects to Roost. Distinct role-scoped identities and mandates can receive tasks, use an admitted local or Codex model, hand off and submit evidence; an independent identity reviews where required. Hello World is only a disposable first-run connectivity check. | A real task is assigned, executed, reviewed and returned to the owner with observed actor/backend/result and recoverable stop; no Roost source-development task is given to the connected agent. RF-GOV-004/006/010, RF-HOST-022/023. |
| Customer and service delivery | A canonical customer and authorized contact connect qualification, offer/scope, accepted engagement, payment condition, project and milestones, tasks, change requests, acceptance, handover and support. Paid discovery and delivery do not start from an informal request. | The owner can trace one engagement, its current commitment, approved work, delivered evidence, payment state and next responsible action. This is a planned capability under RF-SVC-001..012 and RF-BIZ-002/009, not proof of live sales. |
| Economics and care | Capture actual or explicitly unknown effort and cost per task/project and link revenue, invoices and provider state to the customer/product/service. Defects, support cases and incidents connect to responsible work; procedure quality improves from verified outcomes. | Margin or profitability is shown only from known sources, unknowns are visible, and each support issue has status and owner. RF-BIZ-004/005/008/009, RF-SUP-001..004. |

## Interface and data rules

- Navigation favors **Today, Work, Projects/Applications, Customers, People/Agents, Finance, and Settings**; an area is a contextual filter, not a requirement to open thirteen parallel overviews. Show a capability in primary navigation when it has a meaningful owner journey. Otherwise explain what it will do and where to begin, or keep it out of the primary path. Do not render generic record counts as a business dashboard.
- Each page has a clear primary action, meaningful empty state, searchable current work, relevant status, and evidence/next step. Legal, strategy and operations should expose their real questions and workflows; a generic table or empty overview is not acceptance. Validate PL/EN, keyboard, narrow viewport, errors and recovery with signed-in visual checks.
- Inventory production records across **all families** before cleanup. Identify owner/company records, active dependencies, completed audit, disposable test data and unknown provenance. Delete only proven disposable unreferenced records under an exact impact preview; preserve required audit and provider-owned data. Archived records disappear from ordinary active lists and agent default context, while explicit authorized history remains accessible. No blanket reset or recurring seed.
- Treat the 2026-10-14 21:42 UTC Rdzeń report (240 public tables, 138 nonempty; 33 active procedures, 10 proposed Decisions, one technical active Goal, 280 Tasks and 5,231 CompanyRecords) as a read-only inventory snapshot, not a deletion list. The later Today slice preserves the inactive status of matched CompanyRecords and architecture components across repeat `--apply`, with focused native proof. This does not classify other records or complete the current/history journey.
- Track every journey as `accepted requirement -> current implementation -> native proof -> deployed owner/agent proof -> remaining gap` in the existing traceability and implementation documents. A model, route, migration or mock alone is partial evidence. No invented percentages or dated forecast.

## Capability map for a digital-services company

This map prevents an empty department page from being mistaken for a working module. Implement each row as a connected owner/agent journey when its dependencies are ready, not as a new isolated table or mandatory department tile.

| Company capability | Minimum useful job |
| --- | --- |
| Management and strategy | Adopt direction, set measurable outcomes, see exceptions and choose material trade-offs. |
| Products and application engineering | Define a new application or take over an existing one, track assumptions, requirements, defects, versions, evidence and readiness. |
| Operations and quality | Launch reusable procedures into accountable tasks, handle exceptions, inspect results and revise methods. |
| People and agents | Maintain real workers and role profiles, competence, availability, supervision, task mandates and handoffs; catalog roles are not automatically active employees. |
| Knowledge and files | Find current approved company/project sources, provider-owned files and provenance from the task or customer context. |
| Relationships and sales | Know each prospect/customer, authorized contact, need, offer, commitment and next action. |
| Client delivery | Connect paid scope to project, milestone, tasks, deliverables, customer acceptance, changes and handover. |
| Finance | Show cash/invoice/provider status, actual or unknown cost, service/product revenue, obligations and exceptions with source evidence. |
| Legal and compliance | Surface only real contract, permission, privacy, licence or jurisdiction questions tied to an engagement/product and accountable action; a generic graph is not a useful legal workspace. |
| Support and maintenance | Turn customer reports and monitored incidents into classified cases, safe recovery work, follow-up and procedure improvement. |
| Marketing and innovation | Keep research, positioning, experiments and product opportunities linked to approved direction; publishing or customer promises require their own authority. |
| Integrations and administration | Declare authority for ClickUp/Drive and later providers, surface sync conflict/staleness, manage installation health, backup and access without duplicating provider records. |

## Ordering principle

### Dependent company and release branches

| Branch | Entry and proof boundary | Decision before execution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product sale | After an existing application reaches accepted sale readiness, prove the complete customer path in provider sandbox, then separately authorize one live transaction, reconcile payment, application entitlement, invoice, Roost and accounting/file destinations, and start with a controlled cohort. RF-APP-015, RF-BIZ-001/004/005/006/009. | Choose offering, provider and accounting authority, first-sale automation boundary and live financial effects; OPEN-FIN-001. |
| Service sales and delivery | Prospect collection and full service sales/delivery start only after one existing application reaches accepted sale readiness **and** its completion process is reusable. Then prove a governed customer-to-paid-scope-to-acceptance journey. RF-SCOPE-004, RF-SVC-001..012. | Decide offering, pricing, contract and customer authority, payment milestones, jurisdiction-specific terms and access; OPEN-SVC-001..004. |
| Legal and operations | Tie real contract, privacy, licence, access and incident questions to accountable work; prove host/VPS inventory before any separately supervised exact-target maintenance. RF-SEC-002, RF-HOST-021, RF-SUP-001..004. | Decide applicable jurisdiction/contract authority and scoped host access, rollback and budget; OPEN-HOST-001. |
| Public entry and data lifecycle | Audit the pre-login page against an approved audience and truthful claims. Later export/archive/permanent-deletion and integration disconnect require exact scope, retention, recovery and provider effects. RF-UX-011, RF-INT-006. | Approve public promise and UX brief; separately resolve OPEN-DATA-001 before activating the deferred lifecycle requirement. |

### Delivery order

First repair the owner's current work surface and production data hygiene, because every later workflow depends on truthful navigation and records. Then make tasks, decisions, goals and procedures form a usable work loop. Establish both application entry paths and the installable companion with a disposable first-run check. Prove a bounded real company task and role handoff before expanding autonomy. Prove one existing application's sale readiness and a reusable completion process before prospect collection or full service sales/delivery under RF-SCOPE-004; product payment has separate sandbox and controlled live proof. Add customer/service delivery, economics, legal decisions, operations and support as distinct linked slices. Native mobile remains deferred under RF-SCOPE-001 until the web owner loop works reliably, then reuses its backend and canonical records after platform, first workflow and distribution are decided. The next bounded task is selected from actual remaining proof, not from an old numbered gate or this document alone.

## External design references

These are comparison points, not Roost dependencies or a copy of another product. [Odoo Project templates](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/services/project/project_management/project_templates.html) connect reusable stages, tasks and roles to an instantiated project; Roost needs the equivalent traceable launch for its own procedures and tasks. [Odoo milestone invoicing](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/sales/sales/invoicing/milestone.html) links a service commitment to project milestones and invoice readiness; Roost's accepted payment-before-work rule is stricter and remains authoritative. The [ISO process approach](https://www.iso.org/files/live/sites/isoorg/files/archive/pdf/en/iso9001-2015-process-appr.pdf) describes inputs, outputs, responsibility, measurement and improvement; it informs the procedure model without claiming certification or requiring process bureaucracy.
