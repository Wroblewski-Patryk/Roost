# Department signals: bounded Outcome 0 slice

## Scope before edits, 2026-10-15

RF-OUT-001/002 and RF-UX-010/012 require an owner to see relevant work and reach its canonical record without treating a partial metric as company health. On `c810acf7`, `/v1/company-intelligence/health` computes `score` and `healthy/attention/critical` from overdue and blocked Tasks, stale/rejected evidence and broken/missing CompanyRecords only. The shared department strip can therefore say "No active blockers" and show 100% while pending Decisions or Risks exist. It shows at most four positive signals, omitting overdue Tasks and data-quality signals. The only in-repository runtime consumer is this strip; an API test asserts the score and a UI fixture supplies it. This is a bounded contract repair, not whole-console acceptance.

The accepted change will replace the unsupported health percentage with named, source-backed counts and a coverage statement in every department. It will show every positive material signal without silent truncation, keep ordinary open Tasks separate from warning signals, and link to the corresponding scoped canonical work or exact record. Loading, empty and error states must avoid a claim of complete health. No Decision will be classified by title or age. The existing workspace and department boundaries remain authoritative.

Estimated technical work: 8–16 hours, with no hard time cap, paid model call or reset credit. Proof needs a focused native/API case with pending Decision and active Risk alongside the old 100% condition, PL/EN browser checks on narrow and wide viewports with keyboard, empty/error states, independent UX/Security review, `npm run validate`, `npm run codex:check`, one scoped commit/push and at most one standard Coolify deployment after read-only preflight. Production verification is read-only; signed-in PL/EN is conditional on an available owner session. Stop for a substantial new provider or architecture, unsafe production/recovery/capacity state, irreversible data effect, or repeated ineffective attempt. No Worker/model action, production company-data write, portfolio application change, purchase, cleanup or next gate is authorized.
